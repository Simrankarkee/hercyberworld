Scammers reuse the same tricks again and again, so sharing them protects everyone.

**How to post safely:**

1. Take a screenshot or copy the text
2. **Remove** your phone number, name, account numbers and any codes
3. Say where it came from (SMS, WhatsApp, email, call)
4. Don't click the links, and don't post working links. Write `hxxp://bad-site[.]com` instead

Not sure if something is a scam? Ask here and the community will help.
