Online harassment includes threats, repeated unwanted messages, fake profiles impersonating you, sharing private images without consent, and **sextortion**, where someone threatens to share intimate images unless you pay or send more.

If this is happening to you: **it is not your fault, and you are not alone.** Help exists.

## If someone is blackmailing you (sextortion)

1. **Do not pay** and do not send anything more. Paying almost always leads to more demands.
2. **Stop responding**, but don't delete the conversation yet.
3. **Save evidence**: screenshots of messages, usernames, profile links, dates and any payment requests.
4. **Report and block** the account on the platform.
5. **Tell someone you trust** and report to the police. In Nepal, the Nepal Police Cyber Bureau handles cybercrime.
6. If you're worried about intimate images being shared, **StopNCII.org** helps adults create digital fingerprints of images so participating platforms can block them, without you uploading the images. For people under 18, NCMEC's **Take It Down** service does something similar.

## If someone is impersonating you

- Report the fake profile to the platform using its impersonation option
- Ask friends to report it too
- Post on your real account that a fake exists and nobody should send it money

## If you're receiving threats or abuse

- Use **block**, **restrict** and **mute** tools
- Limit who can message, comment on or tag you
- Keep a simple log of incidents with dates and screenshots
- If you feel physically unsafe, contact the police immediately

## Supporting a friend

- Listen without blaming
- Help them save evidence and report
- Don't share or forward the content, even to "prove" it exists
