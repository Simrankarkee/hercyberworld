# Phishing 101

Spot the hook before you bite

---

## What is phishing?

A **fake message** (email, SMS, DM or call) pretending to be someone you trust, so you:

- click a bad link
- share a password or OTP
- send money or gift cards

---

## Red flags

- **Urgency:** "Your account will be closed in 24 hours!"
- **Odd sender:** `support@paypa1-help.com`
- **Links that lie:** long-press or hover to see the real address
- **Too good to be true:** prizes, refunds, jobs you never applied for
- **Asks for secrets:** no real bank asks for your OTP

---

## Where it shows up

- **Email:** fake bank, delivery or job offers
- **SMS ("smishing"):** SIM/KYC updates, parcel fees
- **Calls ("vishing"):** "I'm from your bank…"
- **Social media DMs:** "Is this you in this video?"

---

## Clicked by mistake? Do this

1. Don't type anything else into that page
2. Change the password from a *different* device
3. Turn on 2FA
4. Call your bank if money or cards are involved
5. Warn friends and report the message

---

## Exercises

1. An email from "support@paypa1-help.com" says your account is locked. Name two red flags.
2. A caller says they're from your bank and asks for the OTP you just received. What do you do?
3. You clicked a link and typed your password. What are your first two steps?

---

## Remember

> Pause. Check. Then click.

When in doubt, contact the company **yourself** using their official app or number.
