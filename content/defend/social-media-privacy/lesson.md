# Social Media Privacy Checkup

15 minutes to a safer profile

---

## Why it matters

Public profiles help attackers:

- Guess passwords and security answers (pet names, birthdays, schools)
- Write convincing phishing messages that mention your friends
- Track where you live, work and travel
- Impersonate you to ask your friends for money

---

## Never post publicly

- Your phone number or home address
- Photos of ID cards, passports, tickets or boarding passes
- Live location while you're away from home
- Your children's school name and uniform
- Answers to common security questions

---

## Instagram

1. Settings → **Account privacy** → Private account
2. **Two-factor authentication** → turn on
3. Story → Hide story from people you don't know
4. **Activity status** → off
5. Review **apps and websites** connected to your account

---

## Facebook

1. Settings → **Privacy Checkup** (guided tool)
2. Future posts → **Friends**
3. Who can look you up by phone or email → **Only me** or **Friends**
4. Profile and tagging → **review tags** before they appear
5. Security → turn on **two-factor authentication**

---

## TikTok and others

- Make account **private**
- Limit who can **comment, duet and message** you
- Turn off **suggest your account to others**
- Same rule everywhere: **2FA on**

---

## Exercises

1. Search your own name in a private browser window. What can a stranger see?
2. Which three settings would you change first on your most-used app?
3. A quiz app asks "Your first pet + the street you grew up on = your celebrity name!" Why is that risky?

## Remember

> Post as if a stranger is reading, because one might be.
